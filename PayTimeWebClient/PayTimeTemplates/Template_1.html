Basic HRPolicy & 9 to 6 work schedule(weekend off)

DESCRIPTION: The most basic work schedule for regular office working hours & most basic policy that almost companies are following.

HR Pollicy

Employee Rules:
1. First Week Off : Saturday
2.First Week Off Rule : All
3. Second Week off : Sunday
4.Overtime allow for employees.

Shift Calculation Formula :
1.OUT Punch accepted within 12 hrs.
2.Time Duration between two punches : 3 minutes
3.Grace Time For Late IN : 10 minutes
4.Grace Time For Early OUT: 10 minutes
5.Application Mode : AUTO
6.Monthly Report Start Day : 1st day of the month.

Overtime Calculation Formula:
1.Minimum Hour for OverTime Start : 60 minutes

Attendance Rule :
1.Present at any error case.
2.Half Day & Absend Count Late after Late : 60 minutes
3.Count Early departure after Early Departure : 10 minutes
4.Calculate Half Day If Late Count Greater or equal 3 times
5.Calculate Absent If Late Count Greater or Equal 5 times
6.Total hour Calculation as After shift Start time.

Shift

Shift Name : 9to6 Shift
Short Name: 9T6
Start Time : 9:00 am
End Time : 6:00 pm
Shift Duration : 9 hours
Recess Duration : 1 hour
Min Hrs for Full Day : 9 hours
Min Hrs for Half Day : 4 hours
Absent SMS Schedule Time : In 60 minutes
